Industrial Heat Solutions

Built to Decarbonise Heavy Industry

Antora Energy is a hard-tech company on a mission to eliminate carbon from industrial heat — the world's largest unaddressed source of emissions. We design and deploy thermal battery systems that store cheap renewable electricity and deliver it as continuous, high-temperature heat to factories, mines, and processing facilities around the clock.

Founded by engineers and climate scientists, we believe the energy transition requires physical infrastructure, not just software. Our team combines deep expertise in materials science, power electronics, and industrial operations to build solutions that work in the real world — reliable, scalable, and economically compelling from day one.

  • Antora's thermal battery converts electricity into heat, which is stored in solid carbon blocks at temperatures exceeding 1,800 °C. When industrial heat is needed, the stored energy is released as high-temperature process heat — delivering continuous, dispatchable thermal energy without combustion or emissions.

  • Antora is purpose-built for energy-intensive industries that require continuous, high-temperature heat — including mining and ore processing, cement and lime production, chemical manufacturing, and food processing. Any process demanding sustained temperatures between 300 °C and 1,500 °C is a strong candidate for Antora integration.

  • Antora modules are factory-built and designed for drop-in deployment alongside existing equipment. Our engineering team works directly with plant operators to map thermal load profiles and integrate seamlessly with current heat delivery infrastructure — minimising downtime and capital expenditure during transition.

  • The thermal battery charges from any electricity source — solar, wind, grid power, or a combination of all three. Its ability to absorb intermittent power and deliver firm, 24/7 heat makes it an ideal partner for behind-the-meter renewable generation, helping facilities maximise the value of on-site clean energy assets.

  • Facilities that replace fossil-fuel-fired furnaces or kilns with Antora thermal storage can eliminate up to 80 % of process-related CO₂ emissions. Actual reductions depend on the carbon intensity of the electricity supply and the share of total heat demand served by the thermal battery system.
